Tokens become nearly free

CONFIDENCE · HORIZON [YEAR]

The price of a token has only moved in one direction for as long as I have been watching, and the cuts keep landing on the small model tiers first. That is where most of the everyday work happens, so that is where I expect the effect to show up.

Cheap enough to ignore is a different bar from free. I mean the point where nobody budgets for a call, the way nobody budgets for a page load. Routine agent work gets there first: summarising, sorting, checking, rewriting.

Frontier reasoning is the part I am least sure about. Training runs keep getting more expensive and someone has to recover that cost, so the best models may stay priced like a scarce thing while everything below them goes to nothing.

If that split holds, the claim is true where it matters for most products and false for the headline models. What would change my mind is a year in which the cheap tiers stop getting cheaper and the savings only show up at the top.
