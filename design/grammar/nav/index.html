Decided 4 Nov (Maggie)

  1. Peek: a tethered card or a peek panel Decided: a. It grows from the row you pointed at and covers the least; the panel hides the right side of what you're reading.
  2. Briefing: a rail entry, or the home of the main area Decided: b, revised 4 Nov. Briefing is the first item in the type rail, selected on open, and shows its one column of what changed in the main area. "Aber" is plain text.
  3. Rail order: refined to raw, or raw to refined Decided: a. You go to themes and theories most; the long streams sit at the bottom, where they can grow.
  4. Theme filter on Theories and Signals Decided: a. Themes stay a way in to theories and signals without being the default.
  5. An Unseen group at the top of Signals Decided: b. Briefing already gathers new signals.
  6. Where the view switcher sits in the default view Decided: a. It's the same floating spot as in Columns, Atlas and Timeline, so the switcher never moves when you change views.

The model

  1. Briefing is the first rail item, above Themes, and is selected on open. It shows one column of what changed in the main area, with no second sidebar. "Aber" is plain text, not a link.
  2. Four views: Default (type-first, with Briefing home), then Columns, Atlas and Timeline, unchanged. The switcher moves between them; the last three are today's views with their own index and docked pane.
  3. The rail lists the types with their glyph tiles and counts. A count always means quantity. No new dot on a tile: Briefing shows what is new (decided 4 Nov).
  4. The second sidebar lists one type's items, ordered for its population: themes all visible; theories by Held, Drafts, Retired; prototypes by status; signals and dumps as day streams with search. Row designs are placeholders until 06 Objects.
  5. The selected item fills the main area as its full view. The main area is the focus region, so it pads at pane level (20 / 24).
  6. Every related object has three rungs. Preview: its row, card or chip where it's mentioned. Peek: hover for half a second, or click, to see its essentials with Open; clicking inside a peek peeks the next thing. Full: Open, and the rail and list move to it; the back link returns.
