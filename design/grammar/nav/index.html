The modelRail of types, a list of one type, the item in the main area; three rungs for every object
  1. Four views: Default (type-first, with Briefing home), then Columns, Atlas and Timeline, unchanged. The switcher moves between them; the last three are today's views with their own index and docked pane.
  2. The rail lists the types with their glyph tiles and counts. A count always means quantity. No new dot on a tile: Briefing shows what is new (decided 4 Nov).
  3. The second sidebar lists one type's items, ordered for its population: themes all visible; theories by Held, Drafts, Retired; prototypes by status; signals and dumps as day streams with search.
  4. The selected item fills the main area as its full view. The main area is the focus region, so it pads at pane level (20 / 24).
  5. Every related object has three rungs. Preview: its row, card or chip where it's mentioned. Peek: hover for half a second, or click, to see its essentials with Open; clicking inside a peek peeks the next thing. Full: Open, and the rail and list move to it; the back link returns.
