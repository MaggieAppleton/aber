Decided 4 Nov 2026: cards A soft shadow; peeks and menus B crisp; floating controls A smallest shadow. These tiers, shadows, radii (controls now 7) and z-index tokens are the default on every grammar stage. Which surface sits at which height, and what fill, edge, shadow, radius and z-index each height gets. Already decided: colour system D surfaces, Crisp shadows (a 0.5px ring plus soft drops), radii 4 / 7 / 10 / 14 and pill, concentric; selected is the neutral raise (--sel); hover a 5% layer; press 0.98; drafts dashed; the pane docks with a seam and no shadow; X only closes transient surfaces.

Decide on this page

  1. Cards: soft shadow, hairline only, or fill only
  2. Peeks and menus: quiet, crisp or deep shadow
  3. Floating controls: shadow or flat

The rules (decided 4 Nov 2026)

  1. Every surface has one tier, and the tier sets its shadow, z-index and radius. The table below is the whole set; a new surface type is a decision, not a default.
  2. One meaning per mark. Fill says which plane you're on (ground, sunk, surface). A hairline is an edge between peers on one plane: a docked seam, rows in one container, a flush box or chip. A shadow means "above" and nothing else. Dashed means "not yet mine".
  3. Shadow grows with tier; same tier, same shadow. No shadow on the base or on docked chrome. Shadows come only from the tier tokens (--sh-raised … --sh-tip).
  4. Radius grows with what a surface holds. 4 for marks and tiles; 7 for controls and objects (buttons, chips, rows, cards, tooltip); 10 for boxes that hold rounded things (evidence, peek, menu, toast); 14 for floating bars and modals. Inside another rounded surface: outer minus the inset.
  5. Separate with space first (spacing rule 8). A gap between objects; a hairline only between uniform rows in one container; a seam between docked regions. No lines between sections.
  6. z-index only from tier tokens: 0, 10, 20 … 70 for tiers 0–7.
  7. One transient layer at a time. Opening a peek, menu, modal or toast closes any other; only a tooltip may sit over one.
  8. Inked surfaces are momentary messages. The tooltip and the toast are the only ink-filled surfaces besides the primary button: ink fill, on-ink text, no ring.
  9. Only liftable things lift. Selected raises within its tier (--sel); hover is a 5% layer; press shrinks to 0.98. Nothing lifts on hover.
  10. On a narrow screen the rail and the pane become tier-3 sheets from their own edge; tethered surfaces become bottom sheets.
