Maggie, 4 Nov: linen neutrals + bright types. Ink on linen's quiet off-white, near-black ink and black primary, with Clean light's bright type tints and glyphs. Teal only marks new and focus.

Near-white and barely warm, crisp ink, fresh light type tints and a jade primary. The neutrals almost disappear; colour comes from glyphs, jade and status. Closest to Foglamp.

B Ink on linen

Off-white just warm of neutral, near-black ink and a black primary; quiet dusty tints. Teal only marks new and focus.

C Studio grey (agent's pick)

True neutral, lifted light, with the rail only just below the ground. With no tint in the neutrals, the six type glyphs and jade are the only colour, so they read vividly: calm surface, vivid where it counts.
