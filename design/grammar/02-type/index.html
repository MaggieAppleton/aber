The rules (proposed)

  1. Every piece of text has one role, and each role has one size, weight, ink and line height. If it isn't in the table, it doesn't exist yet: add a role, never a one-off.
  2. Few sizes; tell peers apart with weight and ink first. Body is 13. One size below it, for labels, meta and counts. One size above it, for the pane title. Nothing is smaller than the small size.
  3. Two inks for text. Ink for what you read and act on; ink 2 for what names or describes it. Ink 3 is never text.
  4. Three weights. 600 for the subject (card names, section headers, pane title). 500 for things you press or that name a value (buttons, chips, labels, counts, the current nav item). 400 for everything else.
  5. Line height: 1.4 for interface text, 1.55 for prose, 1.2 for the pane title. Prose stops at 68 characters a line.
  6. Wrap or truncate by role. Names wrap (cards up to three lines, the pane title in full). Nav items, chips and meta truncate to one line with an ellipsis and show the full text in a tooltip. Labels never wrap.
  7. Numbers are tabular wherever they count or compare. Keys and exact times use Geist Mono at the small size.
  8. No uppercase, no letter-spacing, except −0.01em on the pane title.
