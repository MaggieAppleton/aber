Grammar 10: States

Proposed, 4 Nov 2026. Empty, loading, background work, errors, offline, toasts, the one confirmation and success, each shown in the type-first default view and, where it applies, in Columns, Atlas and Timeline. Built only from 00–05 and the decided navigation. The item-list rows, cards and the Add slot are placeholders until 06 Objects; only the state treatments are proposed here.

Decide on this page

  1. Where agent work in progress shows: the composer, the Dumps type, or only the dump
  2. When a dump finishes: a toast, or silent new dots
  3. Where offline shows: beside the view switcher, or in the composer

The rules (proposed)

  1. Empty: say what's absent in one line, and show the action that fills it. One line in the empty-state role (13 / 400 / ink 2), no full stop, no illustration, no instructions.
    • Genuinely empty, made by me (themes, theories, prototypes; a group inside an object): an Add slot where the first item would sit, at its height.
    • Genuinely empty, made by the agent (signals, dumps): one line only; the composer is the action and it's always on screen.
    • Filtered to nothing: the search and filter stay; the line names the query and scope; Clear filters beneath; Esc clears the search.
    • First run: no counts, no sample content; the composer has the caret; Briefing says "Nothing yet".
    • Caught up: Briefing keeps its title and says "Nothing new since 18:40".
  2. Loading: nothing under 300ms, then a skeleton in the content's own geometry; never a layout shift. Chrome that needs no data (rail types, switcher, composer) renders at once, and the composer works before data arrives. Skeleton bars sit at the real row, card and title heights. Canvases draw their frame and fade their marks in. Media of known size keeps its box. Local data (opening an object) has no loading state. Spinners are only for an action you just took, inside its own control (05's Loading state), never for content.
  3. Background work: only work I started shows progress.
    • A dump appears the moment it's sent, at the top of Today, saying Processing in words with the Working spinner; its page shows what I sent.
    • It resolves in place: the status line goes and what it made appears there with new dots. Paused (spend limit) is a hollow ring and the reason, and resumes by itself.
    • Agent work I didn't start (evidence linking, tripwires, suggestions) shows nothing until it produces something; then it appears dashed with a new dot, and Briefing counts it.
    • Where in-progress work also shows outside the dump is choice 1.
  4. Errors sit beside their object, in words, with the way out. Never only in a tooltip, never in a global banner. A red dot is the only colour; the message is ink.
    • Recoverable (a page needs a login, a save failed): the reason and Retry, side by side.
    • Not recoverable (a file type it can't read): the reason and what's still possible, no Retry.
    • A failed dump stays visible in Dumps and on its page, with everything I sent. A failed save keeps my text in the field with "Not saved" and Retry. A list that didn't load says so where the list would be.
    • Copy says what happened and what to do, in plain words; never a raw error message or a code.
  5. Offline is shown once, for as long as it lasts, and changes nothing else. Edits save locally and sync on reconnect. A dump sent offline waits with a hollow ring ("Waiting to send"). Reconnecting shows nothing. Where the one indicator sits is choice 3.
  6. Toasts are tier 6 and carry Undo. Ink, one line, 44 high, radius 10; only the quoted name truncates. They sit just above the composer, right edges aligned, in every view. 5 seconds, held while hovered or focused (then 1.5 more); ⌘Z while one shows. One at a time: a new one replaces the last and commits it; they never stack. They never take focus (role="status"), and never carry an error.
  7. Only Delete dump asks first. A modal (tier 5) with the real counts ("Removes 3 signals and 2 evidence links. It stays in the bin for 30 days."), Cancel, and an ink "Delete dump". Esc and the X close it. No toast after confirming. Everything else that removes (discard, remove, unfile, retire, merge, move to the bin) happens at once with an Undo toast.
  8. Success is mostly silent: the object appearing is the feedback. Accept solidifies a draft in place; saving shows nothing; creating opens the new object; filing shows the chip. A toast appears only when something leaves the screen (and so needs Undo).

A link dump is processing. The dump's own row and page always say so; this is about the one mark you'd see from anywhere else.

A In the composer

A Working spinner at the composer's left while anything you sent is processing; a red dot if one failed. Opens Dumps. Same place in all four views, right where you dumped. What the spec'd pill dot was meant to be.

B On the Dumps type

The spinner takes the Dumps count's place in the rail; a failure is a red dot on its tile. Reads as part of navigation. Columns, Atlas and Timeline have no rail, so they show nothing.

C Only on the dump

No global mark. You see work only in Dumps (and Columns' Dumps strip); the result arrives as new dots. Quietest; a failure waits until you look.

The dump made three signals. Today the app shows a toast with the counts and Undo (which deletes the dump).

A Toast with Undo

"Made 3 signals" above the composer for 5 seconds, wherever you are. Undo removes the dump and what it made. A success toast: the one exception to rule 8.

B Silent: new dots

The status line goes, the signals appear in place, and Signals gets its new dot. No toast; undoing is deleting the dump. Keeps toasts meaning Undo only.
