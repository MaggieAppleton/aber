The rules (decided 4 Nov 2026)

  1. Phosphor only, Regular at rest. Fill marks the current or selected thing (the current view, a pressed toggle) and nothing else. No Light, Bold or Duotone.
  2. Icons are 16, beside 13 body. 14 beside 12px text (chips, card meta, evidence headers, buttons). Nothing smaller than 14. No icon sits beside an 11 count or the 24 title.
  3. Every icon is a square of its size, in a fixed slot. A bare glyph's slot is 16. Every object glyph sits on its pale tile: 24 in rows, headers and chips (flush in the chip's corner), glyph + 4 in card meta. Slots in a list share a left edge, so the text after them lines up.
  4. Centre the slot on the first line of its text. Geist's cap-height centre sits within 0.3px of the line-box centre, so the line box is the reference. Single-line rows centre. Rows that wrap align to the top and drop the slot by (line − slot) ÷ 2: 1 for a 16 icon beside 13 text, −3 for a 24 tile. No hand nudges.
  5. Icon to text is always 6 (--gap-icon). A chip pads 2 less on its glyph side.
  6. Icon-only buttons are 28 squares (--control), 24 inside dense rows (--control-sm), with the 16 glyph centred and a 40 hit area.
  7. Every icon-only button has a tooltip: its name from the vocabulary, then its key in Geist Mono. 12 / 500 on ink, below the button (above in bottom bars), after a 500ms rest.
  8. Give it a text label when the verb is destructive or rare (Retire, Delete), when it's the main decision on a surface, or when it isn't in the vocabulary. Rejecting a suggestion is always a labelled Dismiss text button left of Accept, never an icon (DI-009). Icon-only is for frequent, standard verbs: close, find, add, zoom, views.
  9. Colour has four roles. Ink 2 at rest. Ink on hover and when pressed. Type colour only for object glyphs. Accent only for the current view. Status is a dot, never a coloured icon. Ink 3 is never an icon.
  10. One icon per meaning. The table below is the whole vocabulary. A new meaning gets a new row; it never borrows an icon.

Beside 13 body every icon is 16. The question is the 12px places: chips, card meta, the evidence headers and buttons. Look at the theme chip, Rival, the related chips and the prototype count on the cards.

A One size: 16 everywhere

One number to remember; glyphs read clearly in chips. The glyph is taller than the 12px text beside it.

B Two sizes: 16 beside 13, 14 beside 12

Each icon keeps the same ratio to its text (1.2×), so chips and meta sit quieter. Two sizes to keep straight.

The tile is 24 with the 16 glyph inside. Look at the section headers, the evidence rows, the chips in the pane and the card meta.

A In lists and headers

Tiles mark the start of a row or group: section heads, rail, evidence, tripwire, map labels. Chips and inline meta show the bare glyph.

B Everywhere

Every object glyph sits on its tile, including chips (flush in the corner) and card meta. Most colour; most consistent.

C Never

Bare glyphs only; the glyph colour alone carries the type. Quietest; rows lose a strong left edge.
