Grammar 06b: Confidence

Exploration. Confidence is my stance on a theory, 1 to 5, set by me, with a dated history. Today it's five short dashes on the line below the name, and a larger version plus a sparkline in the full view. You asked for something more concise, possibly on the right of the name. Below are eight encodings, each drawn small (lists, cards, peeks) and large (the full view's setter), all with the same theories.

  • Real cases throughout: Code review becomes the bottleneck at 4, just rose from 3 (2 Nov). Frontier intelligence stays scarce at 2, just fell from 3 (3 Nov). Tokens become nearly free and Agents run continuously at 3, steady. Agents keep context at 4 with a challenge. Local models absorb most everyday agent work is a draft with nothing set.
  • Colour stays two meanings (D): accent for up, red for down. Every mark is ink first; colour only ever doubles what shape or words already say.
  • Setters: point to preview, click or drag to set, or focus and use arrows, Home, End or 1–5. Setting the draft shows the unset state turning into a first stance.

Decide on this page

  1. Small: the mark in rows, cards and peeks Recommended A Ticks: exact at 12px, fixed width so the right-hand column stays straight, and a list of them reads as a tiny bar chart. Trend goes in words in the peek, not on the preview (G is the version that puts it on the preview).
  2. Large: the setter in the full view Recommended A Ticks: the same five ticks at 22 tall, so the thing you set is the thing you scan, with H's step line and "Rose from 3, 2 Nov" beside it as the history. If you want the history to be the setter, H is the richer large.
  3. Position in rows: right of the name, or the line below Recommended right of the name: it's one less line per theory, the marks form a column you can scan, and the challenge dot sits beside them.
