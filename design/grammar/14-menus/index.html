RulesProposed: three kinds on one tier-4 surface; Find first in pickers; focus returns to the trigger
  1. Three kinds, one surface. A menu lists verbs, or the values of one attribute. A picker chooses objects. A popover is a small editor or note anchored to its trigger (07's peek is the main one). All three are tier 4: surface, --sh-pop, radius 10, z 40.
  2. Menu anatomy. Inset 4. Items 32 (decided), padding 0 8, radius 6 (10 − 4). A 16 icon in ink 2, only if some item in the menu has one; 6; the label 13 / 400 ink, verb first; the key at the right in Geist Mono 12 / 500 ink 2. Group labels 12 / 500 ink 2, 24 high. A full-bleed hairline separates groups, never items. Width fits the longest item, 200 to 280.
  3. Destructive last. Alone in the last group, verb and object ("Discard signal"), its vocabulary icon, in ink: red only means an error (10). Reversible verbs act at once with Undo; Delete dump opens its one modal.
  4. Picker anatomy. Inset 4. Find first: 28, the filled well, focused on open, named for its type ("Find a signal"). Then 06 rows at 32: the 18 tile (decided), 8, the name on one line, a short key value at the right in 12 ink 2. Groups: related first (the object's themes, what's on screen), then recent, then the rest; already-linked objects are left out. Width 320, or 360 when rows are sentences. The list stops at 296 and scrolls inside.
  5. Picker states. Empty says why ("Every signal is already linked"). No results: "No signals match “query”". For types I make (themes, theories, prototypes), a last row "Create theme “query”" when no name matches exactly. Signals and dumps have no Create row.
  6. Single and multi. A single picker commits on Return or click and closes. A multi picker (File in theme) lists what's chosen first, keeps its order while open, toggles on Return, Space or click, applies each toggle at once and stays open until Esc or a click away.
  7. The current value shows a 14 ink-2 check in the right slot (before any key), and its glyph fills with the label at 500 (decided 5 Nov), and has focus when its menu opens.
  8. Placement. Below the trigger, start edges aligned, 4 apart; at the window's end edge, end edges align. A picker shrinks its list before it flips; it flips above only when there's more room there. Triggers at the window's foot (the chat's crosshair) open above. It never covers its trigger.
  9. Motion. Grows from the trigger (origin at the trigger's centre on the near edge) from 0.96 and transparent, 160ms on the spring; leaves in 90ms ease-out to 0.98, including after a pick. Reduced motion: a 90ms fade in, gone at once.
  10. One transient at a time. Opening one closes any peek, menu or popover. The trigger keeps its hover fill while open; its tooltip doesn't show. An inline trigger on a key line (a prototype's status) pads 4 above and below and 6 at the sides, cancelled by an equal negative margin: the hover layer grows, the text stays on the key line's baseline and the line never moves.
  11. Keyboard and focus. Return, Space or ↓ on the trigger opens on the current or first item; ↑ on the last. Menus: ↑ ↓ wrap, Home, End, a letter jumps, Return or Space acts; the pointer moves focus with it; opened by a pointer, nothing is marked until you move. Pickers keep focus in Find: ↑ ↓ move the active row, Return picks. Esc, a pick or Tab closes and returns focus to the trigger; a click elsewhere leaves focus where you clicked. Focus rings only for keyboard interaction (decided 5 Nov, now in 05; _shared/modality.js): opened by a click, a menu takes focus on itself and the pointer moves only the hover layer; opened by Return, Space or ↓, the first or current item takes focus with the ring. Roles: menu with menuitem or menuitemradio; a picker is a dialog holding a combobox and a listbox; the trigger has aria-haspopup, aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  12. When not to use one. Two to four always-visible choices stay a segmented control (the view switcher). What must block is a modal (Delete dump; ⌘K Find, whose list uses picker rows). Never a native <select>.
  13. Touch (below 640): bottom sheets. Grab handle, items 44, the same groups and order; a picker's sheet rises to full height with Find at its top. See 12 Narrow.
