Grammar 06: Objects, preview, peek, full

Proposed. Every object in Aber follows one anatomy and appears at three rungs: a preview where it's mentioned (a list row, a card, a chip), a peek that grows from that preview, and a full view that fills the main area. Built only from 00–05 and the decided type-first navigation. The list rows here replace the nav jig's placeholders.

Decide on this page

  1. List rows: two lines, or one Recommended a: theory claims and signal gists are sentences; one line cuts most of them off at 296 wide.
  2. Peek: essentials and relations, or essentials only Recommended a: a peek should answer "what is this tied to" without costing your place.
  3. Full view: prose first, or a fields table Recommended a: the key line under the title carries the same values with no labels; you called the label table noisy (DI-010).

The object grammar

One anatomy, the same slots in the same order at every rung. The tile hangs in its own column; everything else shares the name's left edge (the two guides below).

  1. 1 Tile: the type's glyph on its tile. A signal shows its kind (link, voice, note) on the signal tile. No type word is ever written.
  2. 2 Name: wraps (two lines in rows, three in cards, in full in a peek or full view).
  3. 3 One key value: the attribute that tells this object apart from its neighbours. A draft shows "Draft" here until accepted.
  4. 4 Status: one mark, always directly after the key value: challenge (red) beats a pending suggestion (dashed count) beats new (jade). The peek and full view say what the mark means.
  5. 5 On-demand attributes: the date appears at the right end of the key line on hover; the peek and full view add the rest to the key line.
  6. 6 Attached objects: tripwires and suggestions never stand alone. A mark on the host's preview, one line in its peek, a block with Dismiss | Accept in its full view.
  7. 7 Relations: the peek shows the most important one, up to three rows; the full view shows all of them, as the same rows (chips for themes). Rows remove with a minus on hover.
  8. A row never repeats its group. Prototypes grouped by status drop the status; signals and dumps grouped by day show the day only on hover.

The matrix

Read down a column to see every object at one rung. Every cell comes from the same four components. Hover a row or card to see its date. Click a full view to open it at full size.
