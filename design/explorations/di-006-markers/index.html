Three strategies

These differ in where state lives, not in colour. Every strategy keeps challenge reachable and never relies on colour alone.

AOne gutter

Rule
Every object can carry one mark, always in a reserved 14px gutter that leads its name and is centred on its first line. The gutter is reserved even when empty, so names never shift.
Survives
Challenge (red signpost), new (6px dot, no halo), open (ring). Precedence: challenge, then new, then open; the tooltip names all of them.
Spacing
8px clear on every side. Counts always sit at the far right, never beside a mark.
Cost
Every list loses 22px of width. Only one mark shows, so challenge hides new on the same object.

BRoll up, in words

Rule
No marks on individual items. Containers (sidebar theme, Columns theme, Atlas territory) carry one trailing slot with “3 new” and a signpost with the count of challenged theories. Inside, a “3 new” rule and bold titles split unseen from seen.
Survives
New as a count with a word; challenge as a signpost count on the container and a “Challenged” label on the theory. Open is dropped: the empty section already says it.
Spacing
Pills get their own padding; one slot, always trailing.
Cost
Sidebar theory counts go; they remain on section headings. Text pills add more ink than dots.

CNo persistent marks

Rule
Lists show names and counts only. Attention lives in one place: a Briefing row at the top of the sidebar with one number. Feeds put unseen items first under a “New” day label.
Survives
Challenge as a Briefing item and as the tripped tripwire sentence in the theory. Nothing else.
Spacing
Nothing to crowd.
Cost
A challenged theory looks normal on its card, in Columns and in Atlas until you read Briefing or open it. This adds a sidebar row.

Same contexts, each strategy

Real app classes and CSS throughout; only the markers and their wrappers change. Hover or focus anything. Use Hover + tooltip to force the first row's hover paint and the marks' tooltips.

DI-007: Prototype status colour

The radio group is live: click a segment or use the arrow keys. Each option also shows the Columns prototype list, the theory pane's prototype chips, and the glyph beside a new mark to check the collision. Status green is --ap-pos #1f7a4d; the new dot stays the accent #29a383 and is the only dot, so shape separates them.

  • A fill ramp: one glyph shows progress (outline, half, solid). Fill then means progress for this glyph, while the encoding map says fill means selected; the radio shows selection by its raised segment instead.
  • B words: the clearest and the most text. Green is only on “Building”. The glyph becomes a neutral object icon everywhere.
  • C swap: the smallest change. Building becomes green and built stays ink, so the two never share a colour; idea and shelved are grey and differ only by opacity.
