Expanding a Signal and a Dump (DI-004, DI-005)

This jig rebuilds the current inline expansion from the React source. It compares it with three structures, each shown in the Columns view at the real 290px column, in the theme pane's signal feed, and in the Dumps column. Everything is live: expand, edit the note, file, unfile, discard, follow provenance. Nothing touches the app's data.

“This entire outer component, the one that expands a signal inline, is extremely vibe-coded and poor AI design… when [dumps] are expanded, they're extremely long and hard to read… Both of these need significant rethinks from the ground up.”

DI-004 · Expanded Signal, every reuse

A Signal is a distilled observation with provenance (an origin dump, often an external source). It is filed into Themes and used as evidence for Theories. Expanding it should show those relationships and let Maggie act on them.

DI-005 · Expanded Dump

A Dump is Maggie's raw capture (a voice note, text or links). Agents distil it into Signals. Expanding it should first answer “what did this become?”, and then let her read her own words comfortably.

What's wrong now, read from SignalDetail (panes.tsx:93) and DumpDetail (columns2.tsx:261):

  • The gist appears three times: once in the row, again as a boxed editable copy, and a third time as the source link's label (seed.ts:39 stores the gist as the source title).
  • Every value is a bordered box or chip, with no labels. Theme chips and the origin-dump chip sit in one row, so “filed in” and “came from” look identical.
  • Relationship verbs sit in the object's action bar. Unfile (−) and File (+) are unlabelled icons that each open a modal. Discard sits next to them and fires immediately, with no consequence shown and no undo.
  • “Use as evidence” is missing. The detail shows a signal's theories, but there's no way to link one to a new theory.
  • The origin behaves differently by surface. In Columns it unfolds the dump; everywhere else it opens a read-only modal that scrolls inside itself.
  • The dump shows its whole transcript first, as a single 12px paragraph about 1,300px tall at 250px wide, with newlines collapsed. What the dump made follows as unlabelled links at the very bottom.
  • The expanded row turns grey (--ap-sunk) while its children are white boxes: a card inside a card, quieter than the list it came from.

One grammar, two objects

Signals and Dumps share their rungs but not their contents. A Signal's detail is mostly relationships: where it came from and where it's used. A Dump's detail is mostly its own text, plus what it became. Each rung adds information; nothing is repeated from the rung above.

A Field sheet, in place

The row becomes the object. The gist itself turns editable, and under it a labelled sheet: Note, Themes, Theories, From, Source. Each relationship carries its own verb. Themes has an Edit mode that lists each theme with a labelled Unfile, so chips stay clean at 274px and nothing removes on a stray tap. “From” opens Maggie's passage (marked illustrative, with a toggle to today's data); “Open in dump” jumps there. The foot puts Discard… on the left and Merge… on the right; Discard shows its consequences before it acts.

Long content

The sheet grows with real data; there are no clamps. A long note wraps. The passage is one paragraph and only appears when asked for.

Versus the pane

Uses the same field names and order as the .pn-meta list in a pane doc, so promoting a signal to a pane is the same thing, bigger.

B Select, then read in the docked pane

Rows never expand. Selecting one keeps it to a single line, rings it, and opens the object as a .pn-doc in the existing pane: title, meta, note, what the source says, and the passage from the voice note. Each theme chip has a labelled Unfile beside it, revealed on hover or focus and always shown on touch. The pane's × closes the pane, nothing else. “From” pushes the dump document, and the back crumb returns. Escape closes. At narrow widths the pane becomes a full-screen sheet.

Long content

The best case for long reading: 15px at line-height 1.65 with a 68ch measure, and room for a long note.

Versus the pane

This is the pane. Signals and Dumps become first-class pane objects, like Theories and Themes.

Cost

Scanning several signals means repeated selects. The column stays narrow, but the pane takes about 55% of the width.

C Provenance trail

The expansion reads top to bottom as lineage: what the world said, then what Maggie said about it (her extracted words), then this signal and its note, then where it's filed, then what it's evidence for. Object verbs live in a ⋯ menu, so the trail stays clean. Theme chips follow the same grammar: each opens a small menu with “Open theme” and “Unfile from <theme>”. The quoted words are illustrative; the toggle shows what today's data supports.

Long content

Quotes are the extracted span, so they're short. The full passage and the dump are one click away.

Needs

Agents to record the quoted span for each signal. Today there are only origin dump ids.

Recommendation

Signal: A, the field sheet, everywhere SignalRow is used. Borrow C's idea and make “From” open Maggie's own passage, with the extracted words marked once agents store quote spans; until then, show the dump's opening and “Open dump”. Expansion is the right weight for a signal: it is small, mostly relationships, and read while scanning a list. The sheet removes the duplicate gist and the boxes, labels each relationship, and puts each verb on the thing it changes. One rewrite of SignalDetail fixes Columns, the theme feed, the theory grid, Briefing, Atlas and Chat.

Dump: A in the Dumps column, B as the full read. In place, a dump leads with what it made, then the opening and an explicit reveal. “Open in dump” from a signal outside Columns opens the dump document in the pane (B's dump doc), which replaces the inspectDump modal and its inner scroll. A dump is Maggie's long-form writing; it deserves a reading surface, and a signal doesn't need one.

Shared grammar: yes, by rungs, not by layout. A row, then a labelled sheet, then the source on demand. The same field names appear in pane meta. Signal and Dump keep different sheets.

Action placement: relationship verbs on their fields; object verbs at the sheet's foot as labelled buttons; Discard always shows its consequences inline, with Undo afterwards.

Open questions

  • Quote spans. The schema stores the origin dump ids for a signal (signal_origins), but not which words it came from. Showing “From” as Maggie's passage needs agents to record a span or a quoted string.
  • Source titles. seed.ts:39 sets each source's title to the gist. Real sources need the post text or title and the author, or the Source field repeats the gist again.
  • markRead on expand. Today, expanding a row marks it read. Should a glance count, or only opening the passage, or editing?
  • Discard: confirm, undo, or both? The jig does both. One of them is probably enough if agents never re-extract a discarded signal.
  • One open row per column? The jig matches the app (one per column). Comparing two signals may want two open at once.
  • Should Signals and Dumps become pane objects (B), even if in-place expansion stays the default?
