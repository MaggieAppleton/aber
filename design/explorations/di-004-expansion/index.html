Expanding a Signal and a Dump (DI-004, DI-005)

This jig rebuilds the current inline expansion from the React source. It compares it with three structures, each shown in the Columns view at the real 290px column, in the theme pane's signal feed, and in the Dumps column. Everything is live: expand, edit the note, file, unfile, discard, follow provenance. Nothing touches the app's data.

“This entire outer component, the one that expands a signal inline, is extremely vibe-coded and poor AI design… when [dumps] are expanded, they're extremely long and hard to read… Both of these need significant rethinks from the ground up.”

DI-004: Expanded Signal, every reuse

A Signal is a distilled observation with provenance (an origin dump, often an external source). It is filed into Themes and used as evidence for Theories. Expanding it should show those relationships and let Maggie act on them.

DI-005: Expanded Dump

A Dump is Maggie's raw capture (a voice note, text or links). Agents distil it into Signals. Expanding it should first answer “what did this become?”, and then let her read her own words comfortably.

What's wrong now, read from SignalDetail (panes.tsx:93) and DumpDetail (columns2.tsx:261):

  • The gist appears three times: once in the row, again as a boxed editable copy, and a third time as the source link's label (seed.ts:39 stores the gist as the source title).
  • Every value is a bordered box or chip, with no labels. Theme chips and the origin-dump chip sit in one row, so “filed in” and “came from” look identical.
  • Relationship verbs sit in the object's action bar. Unfile (−) and File (+) are unlabelled icons that each open a modal. Discard sits next to them and fires immediately, with no consequence shown and no undo.
  • “Use as evidence” is missing. The detail shows a signal's theories, but there's no way to link one to a new theory.
  • The origin behaves differently by surface. In Columns it unfolds the dump; everywhere else it opens a read-only modal that scrolls inside itself.
  • The dump shows its whole transcript first, as a single 12px paragraph about 1,300px tall at 250px wide, with newlines collapsed. What the dump made follows as unlabelled links at the very bottom.
  • The expanded row turns grey (--ap-sunk) while its children are white boxes: a card inside a card, quieter than the list it came from.

One grammar, two objects

Signals and Dumps share their rungs but not their contents. A Signal's detail is mostly relationships: where it came from and where it's used. A Dump's detail is mostly its own text, plus what it became. Each rung adds information; nothing is repeated from the rung above.

DI-004/005: Provenance popover

Maggie, on the dump viewer: “It shouldn't be a modal, but this inline is very noisy. I think I want to explore popovers for a lot of this inline stuff… a popover to appear next to the signal or below it with more information, like where it came from.” What the provenance consists of, and its order, stays as C settled it: the source, her words in the dump, this signal, filed in, evidence for. This question is only about how a row shows that detail. It sits alongside the B pane, which is still where a signal opens in full (section 7).

Each popover option is the real Columns layout, with every row live. Try Memory feature withdrawn after privacy complaints, the fifth signal. A popover opens on click, Enter or Space; Escape returns focus to its trigger; an outside click or the trigger closes it; only one is ever open. Popovers sit in the top layer with fixed positions, so the scrolling column can't clip them. At 390px each popover stays tethered to its row as a full-width card (12px gutters) below it, or above it when there's no room below. It is not a bottom sheet, because the provenance should stay visibly attached to the signal it explains.

  • C inline trail: everything at once, no extra layer, but the row grows by about 600px and pushes the list; that's the noise Maggie named.
  • A chain below: the full lineage in one place, the list never moves, and one trigger per row. It's tall (about 520px) and covers the rows beneath.
  • B passage beside: answers “what did I actually say?” first and keeps the list in view, but covers the next column; at 390px it falls back to a card under the row.
  • C′ origin parts: small and focused, and each answer is one click. Two triggers per row adds visual weight, and there's no single view of the whole chain.

Open questions

  • Quote spans. The schema stores the origin dump ids for a signal (signal_origins), but not which words it came from. Showing “From” as Maggie's passage needs agents to record a span or a quoted string.
  • Source titles. seed.ts:39 sets each source's title to the gist. Real sources need the post text or title and the author, or the Source field repeats the gist again.
  • markRead on expand. Today, expanding a row marks it read. Should a glance count, or only opening the passage, or editing?
  • Discard: confirm, undo, or both? The jig does both. One of them is probably enough if agents never re-extract a discarded signal.
  • One open row per column? The jig matches the app (one per column). Comparing two signals may want two open at once.
  • Should Signals and Dumps become pane objects (B), even if in-place expansion stays the default?
