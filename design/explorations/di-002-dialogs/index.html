DI-002 · Dialogs

Every dialog in Aber goes through one ask() host: a 480 px box at 20% from the top, a 13 px title, and the close control as a small X beside the primary button at the bottom right. This jig takes each creation dialog out of the modal and into the object it makes, and gives the few modals that stay a shadcn‑style anatomy.

“We should never have a close button that is not in the top-right corner for a modal. I also tend to avoid modals wherever I can… If I'm creating an object, it should look like the final object.”
  1. A modal's close button is always top‑right.
  2. Avoid modals wherever possible. A tethered menu, the pane, or the row itself first.
  3. Creating an object looks like the final object. No isolated form.

Every stage below is the app's real markup (captured DOM) styled by the app's real CSS. Proposal styles use --ap-* and --size-px-* tokens; the one temporary value is the 15 px modal title. Click the real triggers inside each stage. Esc closes. The pills under each stage force a state.

NowCurrent: ask() dialog

X sits next to Create at the bottom; 13 px title; no context. From a theory, the new prototype isn't linked to it.

Ashadcn anatomy modal

Header (title + context), X top‑right, one field, footer with Cancel + Create. Centred, scale 0.95 → 1 in 200 ms, exits in reverse in 150 ms.

  • + One anatomy for every modal that stays; the cheapest change (restyle the one DialogHost).
  • − Still an isolated form that doesn't look like a prototype, against rule 3.
  • − Status, Tests and notes still need a second stop on the page.
BCreate in the docked pane

The pane opens on a blank prototype page: name focused, status idea, Tests prefilled from the theory, notes ready. A row grows in the sidebar and mirrors the name as you type. Saved once named. Closing an untouched one discards it silently. Closing one with notes but no name asks for a name, with Discard beside it.

  • + The thing you make is the thing you see, with every field in reach (rule 3).
  • + No new surface: the pane already slides from the right and closes top‑right.
  • − Replaces whatever was open in the pane (the theory you came from becomes a chip on Tests).
  • − Needs a rule for a nameless prototype (shown: “Name it to keep it”).
CName it at the trigger, then open

The “New prototype” row turns into the new prototype's row with a name field. Enter commits and opens its page in the pane with the notes focused. From a theory, the chip in the theory's links becomes the new prototype in place, and the theory stays open.

  • + Fastest; the list grows by the row you're typing in, as in Linear or Finder.
  • + From a theory you keep your place: it's created and linked without leaving.
  • − Two steps for a full page (name, then pane).
  • − Empty Enter only nudges; nothing to explain why.

Open questions

  1. When does a new object exist? B/C assume it's saved as soon as it has a name, and a nameless, untouched one is discarded silently. Should a nameless one with notes be kept as “Untitled” instead of asking?
  2. Not a draft. “Draft” and the dashed outline mean “the agent made it, not yet mine”. A prototype I'm still naming is mine, so B and C use solid material and the verb Discard, not “Discard draft”.
  3. Validation colour. Red means a challenge, so a missing name shows as ink‑2 text plus a nudge, not a red field. OK?
  4. Superseded by what? Retiring as superseded could ask for the replacing theory (a rival link). Out of scope here.
  5. Modal title size. A uses a temporary 15 px title for hierarchy; the scale has 13 and 20. Add 15 to the scale or keep 13 and rely on weight?
