DI-008: the draft theme heading

Robotics is a draft theme: the agent created it from a voice note (createDraftTheme, demo-seed.ts:41), with one draft theory inside it (which links a draft prototype, Recovery rehearsal) and two signals filed. It is not a suggestion. It becomes mine when I accept or rephrase it; discarding removes it.

Every stage below uses the app's real CSS. Hover, focus, accept (A), rephrase (R), Discard (button only, no key, per DI-009) and Undo are live; Reset replays. The long-named second draft is hypothetical, added to test truncation.

What's wrong now (measured on the live app at 1440)

  1. The box breaks the column edge. The heading button is pulled 6px left so its text lines up; the 1px dashed outline then sits 2px further out, so the box starts at x=252 while cards and every other heading start at x=260.
  2. The count is jammed against it. 2px from the dashed box to “2 signals”, against 16px from name to count on mine headings.
  3. It's a different material from every other draft. Outward 28% black outline, no fill, 26px tall in a 28px row, so it reads as a focus ring or form field. Draft cards use an inset 12% dash with a translucent fill.
  4. Hierarchy is inverted. The draft name stays full ink at 700 while draft claims step down to ink-2.
  5. No decision where it's seen. Accept and discard exist only in the pane (Accept button + X, which DI-009 now rules out); draft cards act from their own edge with X / check minis.
  6. No provenance. Nothing says which dump made it or when.
  7. The sidebar can't tell. Robotics' index row is identical to mine rows apart from the new dot.

AAligned heading, provenance line with the decision

The heading row is byte-for-byte a mine heading. The draft material (inset dash, translucent fill, ink-2) sits exactly where every heading already paints its hover. Below it, one 12px line: where it came from, then Rephrase, Discard, Accept (DI-009: labelled Discard left of the primary Accept). Accepting collapses that line and the heading is simply mine. Dial: “Box aligned” trades text alignment for a box flush with the cards.

BThe whole draft section is one dashed object

The container paints 12px into the gutter so the heading and cards keep the column edge. Rephrase, then labelled Discard and Accept, sit on its top edge on hover or focus (always on touch). Provenance is a mic glyph with a tooltip. Accepting fades the container; the theory inside stays a draft.

CDrafts gathered after my themes as proposal rows

My themes stay uninterrupted; draft themes collapse to one dashed row each (summary before detail), with provenance and labelled actions. The chevron unfolds its theories in place. Accepting moves the row up into my themes as a full section, as does the sidebar row.

  • Current: smallest change, but every finding above stands.
  • A: perfect heading alignment and the clearest provenance; costs one extra 28px line per draft theme until decided. The dashed box still outdents 6px (the hover footprint) unless the dial says otherwise.
  • B: strongest “this whole thing isn't mine yet”; draft theory cards become dashed-inside-dashed, and actions are hidden until hover. Clearly an object, but the heaviest on the page when overnight clustering makes several.
  • C: scales best (many drafts stay one row each) and keeps my sections calm; the draft lives away from where it would eventually sit, and it's a new grouping on the base plane.
