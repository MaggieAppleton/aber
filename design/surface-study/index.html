Crisp, Sage and Jade

Aber\'s surface language, staged across every component and state. The grammar, spatial model and density are unchanged from the design model. Colours are Radix Colors 3.0.0 steps, and the same tokens drive the jig.

Stages

Every component in its states, staged the way it appears in Aber: one state story per shot, real words only on what the shot is about, quiet bars for the rest.

Live

The whole frame, interactive. Click cards to open them, hover a draft to accept or discard it, set confidence, step the year, unfold a signal, open the ⋯ menu, switch views, and click the pill to grow the composer (type a "?" to see send turn into ask). Esc closes the last thing opened.

Hover and press use the short duration so frequent actions never wait. Anything that moves through space (pane, composer, view highlight, toast) uses the spring and can be interrupted mid-flight. Reduced motion swaps all of it for a cross-fade.

Foundation

Spacing is a 4 px grid, which is how density stays where it was. Radii are concentric: an inner radius is the outer radius minus the padding between them.

Spacing · 4 px grid

Nine steps replace 158 padding values. Rows 30, controls 28, cards pad 12.

Radius · five steps

Floating chrome uses the largest step; its buttons take it minus the 6 px inset.
